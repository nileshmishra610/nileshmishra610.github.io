    PhD Research

    My PhD at IIT Jodhpur, supported by the IndiaAI Fellowship, is about teaching machines to understand the Earth in 3D. I combine what satellites see over wide areas with the fine geometry captured by drones and LiDAR, so that large terrains can be reconstructed as digital twins, monitored over time and analysed at scale. These are the directions the work spans:

    Multimodal Geospatial Fusion

    Cross-modal deep learning that combines multispectral satellite imagery with high-density UAV LiDAR and photogrammetry.

    Cross-modal attentionSatellite + LiDARMulti-scale analysis

    3D Terrain Reconstruction & Digital Twins

    High-fidelity, large-scale 3D terrain models and digital twins that hold up under clouds, dense vegetation and complex urban scenes.

    Neural radiance fieldsDiffusion 3D priorsDigital twins

    Point Cloud Understanding

    Filtering and semantic segmentation of 3D point clouds to separate terrain, vegetation, buildings and water.

    Point transformersGraph neural networksSemantic segmentation

    Spatio-Temporal Change Modelling

    Capturing how terrain evolves across seasons and years, from land-cover change to erosion and landslide risk.

    Change detectionLandslide susceptibilityDisaster resilience

    Label-Efficient Learning

    Self-supervised and weakly-supervised methods that learn from large unlabelled geospatial archives with minimal annotation.

    Self-supervised learningWeak supervisionGeneralisation

    Geospatial Intelligence

    Turning 3D reconstructions into usable insight through interactive multi-scale visualisation and analysis tools.

    Remote sensing3D visualisationDecision support
